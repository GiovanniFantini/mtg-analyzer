THE DRAGON IS THE TREASURE


Smaug™, the most infamous Dragon of Middle-earth, reigns supreme as the gleaming gold headliner card. Majestic, terrible, and dazzling in equal measure, the brave (and fortunate) will find one of around 500 printed in Collector Boosters.



Gleaming Gold Smaug the Magnificent cards are localized in English only and are available in Magic the Gathering | The Hobbit™ Collector Boosters of any language. Gleaming Gold Smaug the Magnificent cards are mechanically identical to other Smaug the Magnificent card treatments. Images are digital renderings, not actual cards.
