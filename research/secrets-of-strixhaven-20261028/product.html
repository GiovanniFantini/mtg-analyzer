Find your place among the five colleges as we put down the books and delve into the real world! Uncover the Secrets of Strixhaven and prove your college is the best with every spell you cast.

Strixhaven Quiz

Find out which college you belong to!

As you enter your Second Year, it's time to declare a college! Let’s find out which of the colleges you’ll be studying alongside. Will it be the nature-oriented Witherbloom? The creative flame Prismari? Are you part of Quandrix's equation? A confident and charismatic Silverquill? Or one of Lorehold's adventurous historians? Find out now, no Number 2 pencil required!

Silverquill | College of Eloquence

Sharp Style. Sharper Wit.

When it comes to knowing it all, we actually do…or at least that's what we have others believe. In Silverquill, we're known for our snark, charisma, and competitive edge. We're the first to sign up for the debate team, and we've been practicing our honor student speeches since we arrived. Be prepared to bring wit, finesse, and confidence—and to be on the best-dressed list while doing it.

Prismari | College of Elemental Arts

Express Yourself with the Elements

Prismari are the quintessential expressers of all things creative, beautiful, and elemental. From paint to theater, music to dance, Prismari college believes in self-expression and creativity in all forms. When you're in Prismari you gain friendships for a lifetime (and probably some paint-stained clothes).

Witherbloom | College of Life and Death

Get Your Hands Dirty

Witherbloom college is more than meets the eye. We focus our energy on studying the essence of living beings…whether that's side-eyeing classmates, riding zombie crocs, or picking herbs for experimental potions. The natural world is all about give and take, and in Witherbloom, students give their all and are loyal to a fault.

Lorehold | College of Archaeomancy

Leave No Stone Unturned

With a little bit of mystery and a whole lot of heart, Lorehold is the researching college that's always up for an adventure! Some might see the past as a rich tapestry worthy of study. But in Lorehold, we wear that tapestry like a cape and jump into a forgotten tomb.

Quandrix | College of Numeromancy

Math Is Magic

In Quandrix, students study patterns, fractals, and symmetries. We love intellectual challenges and might show off on occasion with our math genius. After all, studying magical properties is more than a science, it's a code—and Quandrix is here to crack it.

Silverquill Influence Commander Deck

SILVERQUILL INFLUENCE
Weaponize your words with Silverquill standout Killian, who encourages the rest of the table to attack targets that aren’t you. Tap, goad, and control creatures and savor the mayhem.

Prismari Artistry Commander Deck

PRISMARI ARTISTRY
Let your creativity run wild with Rootha as your artist-in-residence and cast expensive, bombastic spells. Bigger spells let you generate bigger Elementals, so pull out all the stops!

Witherbloom Pestilence Commander Deck

WITHERBLOOM PESTILENCE
There’s trouble brewing when Dina leads your Witherbloom forces, so prepare the Pests for sacrifice! Sacrifices grant life, cards, and stackable counters so you can crush the competition.

Lorehold Spirit Commander Deck

LOREHOLD SPIRIT
Set out on a Lorehold expedition with Quintorius! Create Spirits when cards leave your graveyard, then pile on typal Spirit bonuses and buffs to bury your opponents!

Quandrix Unlimited Commander Deck

QUANDRIX UNLIMITED
Formulate the equation for victory with Zimone as your constant variable. Cast X-spells for +1/+1 counters and ever-increasing discounts, supercharging your brainy brawling!

EMULATE THE MAGIC GREATS


Many honors await Strixhaven’s best students, but one outshines them all. As you pursue your education, look for the highest Strixhaven honor: one of 500 serialized Emeritus of Ideation cards. Illustrated by renowned Power Nine artist Mark Poole, this card is a callback to his best-known card, Ancestral Recall.



Serialized Emeritus of Ideation cards are localized in English only and are available in Secrets of Strixhaven Collector Boosters of any language. Non-serialized Emeritus of Ideation cards found in Secrets of Strixhaven are mechanically identical to their serialized variants. Images are digital renderings, not actual cards.

REDISCOVER STRIXHAVEN

What wonders do the hallowed halls have to offer? This year, uncover mysteries on and off campus. As you take it all in, look for special treatments like extended art, special guest cards, borderless field notes, or even Mystical Archive or Japanese silver scroll foil cards!

Product Lineup

Play Boosters

Study ancient battlefields and monstrous mires as you explore the Secrets of Strixhaven. Look for a Mystical Archive spell in every booster!

Collector Boosters

Discover delights in the back of the stacks, like special Japanese-language Mystical Archive cards in Silver Scroll foil, beautiful Borderless cards, or Special Guests cards!

Draft Night

Dorm game night? This Pick-Two, Draft-in-a-Box kit encourages fast, dynamic drafting for four. Crown the winner with the included Collector Booster (or keep it for yourself).

Bundle

Be ready for school with a box with 9 Play Boosters, 10 Traditional Foil lands, an alt-art Traditional Foil Wisdom of Ages card, and an oversized spindown life counter.

Codex Bundle

Stuffed with all the extra credit any student could want, the Codex Bundle includes 2 Collector Boosters, 6 Play Boosters, 2 of 6 Foil promo cards, a special spindown, and more!

Commander Decks

Dive into Magic’s most popular multiplayer format with five 100-card decks–each with a unique two-color play style. Choose your college of magical study and expand your education!

Prerelease Pack

Attend a Prerelease event and be one of the first to uncover the Secrets of Strixhaven. This kit includes everything you need to play, a promo card, and a spindown die.
