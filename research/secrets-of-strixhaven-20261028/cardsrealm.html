What is the set Secrets of Strixhaven?
Secrets of Strixhaven is a Magic: the Gathering expansion with 277 cards in its catalogue, including 261 cards appearing here for the first time and 16 reprints. Among its most played cards are Erode, Emeritus of Ideation // Ancestral Recall, and Petrified Hamlet.
