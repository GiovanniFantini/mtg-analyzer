Dare to enter the ever-changing plane of Lorwyn and Shadowmoor and discover your true self.

AVAILABLE NOW

DISCOVER A SERIALZED BITTERBLOOM BEARER


Explore the forever day and eternal night of Lorwyn Eclipsed and you may find one of 500 serialized versions of Bitterbloom Bearer, illustrated by beloved artist Rebecca Guay in a special borderless showcase treatment. Available only in Collector Boosters.

GET THE BEST OF BOTH WORLDS

KINDRED POWER

Elves, merfolk, kithkin, boggarts, elementals, and more—creature types thrive across Lorwyn and Shadowmoor. Fill your deck with your favorite kindred spells and discover powerful new creature strategies and gameplay.

DISCOVER CREATURES

COMMAND ORDER AND CHAOS

Lead your forces with Ashling or Auntie Ool! Two legendary Commanders, two sides of Lorwyn’s duality. Embrace the power of harmony or harness the potential of blight at your next Commander night.

DIVE INTO COMMANDER

ENCHANTED WITH NOSTALGIA

Throwback cards rekindle the magic of the original Lorwyn block, featuring beloved creature types, classic art, and reimagined cards that celebrate your return to the land of light and shadow.

CLASSIC ART AWAITS

EVERY LIGHT…

Cross the boundry into the eternal day of Lorwyn, where the many creatures live in order and harmony under the watchful eye of their sun god Eirdu—while the twilight god Isilu resides across the border where day becomes night.

Images are digital renderings, not actual cards.

…CASTS A SHADOW

Brave the twisted landscape and wild Magic of Shadowmoor, a world cast in forever night and filled with creatures of a more chaotic ilk. Discover your true self under the perpetual moonlight of this dual-aspect plane.

Product Lineup

Play Boosters

The best way to open and explore Lorwyn Eclipsed. Balanced for Limited play, filled with creature synergies, and a guaranteed foil in every pack for your collection.

Collector Boosters

Get the best of both worlds with Rares, Mythics, foils, and Full-Art lands. The only way to open Japan showcase cards, special showcase treatments, or even a serialized Bitterbloom Bearer.

Commander Decks

Pick up and play Magic’s most popular multiplayer format. Two preconstructed decks that embody the duality of light and shadow, with new legendary commanders tied to Lorwyn or Shadowmoor.

Draft Night

It's a draft party in a box! Draft Night has everything you need to host a Pick-Two Draft with just four players! Bring Lorwyn Eclipsed to the table for an unforgettable game night.

Bundle

Includes 9 Play Boosters, foils, a promo card, and a themed spindown die. Everything you need to play and display the world of Lorwyn Eclipsed.

60-Card Theme Decks

Accessible, ready-to-play decks filled with iconic Magic themes. A fun, fast entry into Standard format—choose a theme that fits you and jump right in.

Prerelease Pack

Be among the first to dive into Lorwyn Eclipsed. Includes Play Boosters, a foil promo card, and a spindown—everything you need for an memorable early play experience.

Draft Night


It’s a draft party in a box! Draft Night is the self-contained kit for hosting a Pick-Two Draft for four players. Pick-Two encourages fast, dynamic drafting that’s great for kitchen table play or adding a dash of variety to game night! Crown the winner with the packed-in Lorwyn Eclipsed Collector Booster (or you can keep it for yourself, we won’t judge).
